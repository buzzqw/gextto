Pick an indexer to enable the search; leave it empty to add one later from the RSS page. Prowlarr and Jackett fill the URL with their usual local address and port. For any other Torznab service (a MirCrew indexer, a remote manager) choose Other and type its URL.

The download folder and the peer port apply at the next start.
