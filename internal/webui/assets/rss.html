RSS & indexers

Feeds fill the archive; rules decide what to download, in order: the first match wins. Indexers answer searches and wanted titles.

Feeds

A feed is an RSS URL, or the page of an ext.to or TorrentGalaxy listing. Its items are fetched periodically, kept in the archive and evaluated by rules and wanted titles.

    {{FEEDROWS}}

Rules

With at least one rule, only items matching a rule are added: a Skip rule rejects, an Add rule accepts with its save path, category, tags and flags. Rules are evaluated top to bottom.

    {{RULEROWS}}

Indexers

Torznab endpoints of Prowlarr, Jackett or another manager. They are searched on demand and for the wanted titles, and their results are archived.

    {{INDEXERROWS}}
