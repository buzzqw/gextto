Rules

With at least one rule, only items matching a rule are added: a Skip rule rejects, an Add rule accepts with its save path, category, tags and flags. Rules are evaluated top to bottom.

What are rules for?

A rule downloads by itself what your feeds publish: think of it as a more powerful "add a series to Wanted". You choose which feeds it watches, which titles it takes and where it saves them.

  • Follow a series only in the quality you want: Include "1080p", Episode filter or Smart episode, so every episode is taken once.
  • Take everything a feed publishes into one folder, with its category and tags.
  • Reject what you never want (cam, a language, too small or too big) with a Skip rule placed above the others.

Before saving, Preview matches in the editor shows what the rule would take from the archive. Without rules a feed only fills the archive, unless its own Include/Exclude select something.
