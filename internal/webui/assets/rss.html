{{tr "A rule downloads by itself what your feeds publish: think of it as a more powerful \"add a series to Wanted\". You choose which feeds it watches, which titles it takes and where it saves them."}}

  • {{tr "Take everything a feed publishes into one folder, with its category and tags."}}
